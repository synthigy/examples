03 · the same in all four

Under every example

One instance

The four apps share one Synthigy engine and its users; each deploys its own model.

A server-side BFF

Tokens never reach the browser. It receives HTML and SSE patches, nothing else.

No build step

Tyrell components and Datastar come from a CDN; the BFF runs as it is written.

A guided tour

Every app walks you through itself, then opens the modeler, console and logs on its data.

Every example is open source: synthigy/examples Synthigy examples · components by tyrell
